Terms

What you can expect from us, and what we expect in return.

Last updated 18 September 2026.

Who these terms are between

ILM — The ILM Network — is operated by Azality. These terms apply to the school that holds the account, to the staff who sign in under it, and to parents and students using the portal. Where a school has signed a separate written agreement with us, that agreement wins wherever the two disagree.

The account belongs to the school

A school's account, and everything recorded in it, belongs to the school. We hold and process it on the school's instructions. The school decides who gets access, at what level, and is responsible for keeping that list current — particularly for removing staff who leave.

Each person's sign-in is personal to them. Passwords and PINs are not to be shared; a shared login makes the record of who did what meaningless, and that record is often the point.

What the school is responsible for

  • That it is entitled to record the information it enters about students, parents and staff
  • That the information it enters is accurate, and corrected when it is not
  • Telling families what the school records and why — we support this, but the relationship with the family is the school's
  • Collecting any consent its own local law requires, including for optional details such as photographs or medical information
  • Only recording sensitive information where the school genuinely needs it

What we are responsible for

  • Running the service with reasonable skill and care, and keeping it available
  • Handling personal information as described in our Privacy page
  • Not selling personal information, and not using a school's data to advertise to anyone
  • Telling a school without delay if we discover a breach affecting its data
  • Returning or deleting a school's data when it leaves, as described in Data deletion

Acceptable use

Don't use ILM to store information you have no right to hold, to harass anyone, to attempt to reach records outside your own role, to probe or attack the service, or to resell access without our agreement. We may suspend an account that is being used this way, and will say why.

Availability

We aim to keep ILM available during school hours and to schedule disruptive work outside them. We do not promise uninterrupted service: it depends in part on providers we do not control, and on the internet between you and us. We will not treat a school's own connectivity problems as our outage, and we would ask the same honesty in return.

Fees

Pricing is agreed with each school in writing before the account starts. Pilot and trial arrangements are set out separately and may be free for an agreed period. We will give reasonable notice before any change to a school's pricing.

Your content

A school's records remain the school's. We claim no ownership of them and use them only to provide the service, to keep it secure, and to fix faults. We do not use a school's student data to train machine-learning models.

The software, design and brand are ours and stay ours.

Ending it

A school can stop using ILM at any time; we will export its records on request and then delete or return them as described in Data deletion. We may end an account for non-payment or serious misuse, with notice and an opportunity to put it right except where the misuse is severe.

Liability

ILM is a record-keeping tool. It does not replace a school's own judgement, its statutory records, or its duty of care. Decisions made using information in ILM remain the school's decisions.

Nothing here limits liability where the law does not allow it to be limited. Otherwise, and to the extent the law permits, neither side is liable to the other for indirect or consequential loss, and our total liability in any twelve-month period is limited to the fees paid by that school for that period.

Changes to these terms

If we change these terms in a way that materially affects a school, we will tell the school in advance. Continuing to use ILM after a change means accepting it. The date at the top always reflects the current version.

Law
