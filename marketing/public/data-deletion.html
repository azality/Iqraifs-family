Data deletion

How to have information removed from ILM, who to ask, and what actually happens after you ask.

Last updated 18 September 2026.

Start with the right people

If your child is at a school using ILM, ask the school first. The school owns the record and can act immediately — we hold it on their instructions, and for anything short of closing the whole account they are both faster and the correct decision-maker. If they need us, we will help them.

If you use the family app on your own, without a school, you are in charge of your own records and can write to us directly.

How to ask us

Email hello@azality.com with the subject Data deletion, and tell us:

  • Who you are, and your relationship to the record — parent, staff member, school, or an individual family user
  • The school's name, if it involves a school
  • What you want removed — a specific child, a parent record, your own staff account, or everything

We reply within two working days, and complete verified requests within 30 days. We will ask a question or two to confirm you are who you say you are — we will not delete a child's record on an unverified email, because that would itself be a way to attack a family.

What gets deleted

For a student: their profile, attendance, marks and exam results, homework submissions, behaviour notes, Quran progress, fee records and payments, exam syllabus entries, and their portal sign-in.

For a parent: their contact details, the link to their children, their messages with the school, and their portal sign-in.

For a family app account: the children, every logged entry, points, rewards, quests and wishlist items.

For a whole school: everything belonging to that school, across every one of the above.

What we may keep, and why

We would rather tell you this plainly than surprise you later:

  • Records the school must keep by law. Schools have their own retention duties — attendance and examination records in particular. Where a school tells us it is legally required to retain something, we keep it for the school and delete the rest.
  • Financial records. Fee and payment entries may need to be retained for accounting and audit. Where they are, we strip them back to the minimum needed and remove the personal detail around them.
  • Backups. Deleted information disappears from the live service straight away, but can persist in encrypted backups for up to 90 days before those backups age out. It is not restored to the live service, and is not used for anything.
  • Aggregate counts. Numbers that no longer identify anyone — how many students a school has, for example — may remain.

Leaving a school is not deletion

When a student leaves, schools normally mark them as having left rather than erasing them, so the school keeps an accurate history and can re-admit them later without losing their record. If you want a record actually deleted rather than archived, say so explicitly — to the school, or to us.

If a school stops using ILM

We delete or return that school's data within 90 days of being asked, other than anything the school tells us it must retain by law. A school can ask for an export of its own records at any time while its account is active.

Instagram

If a school connected an Instagram account to its public website, disconnecting it in the school's settings removes our access and the stored connection. We keep no copy of the school's Instagram content afterwards. You can also revoke access from your own Instagram account settings.
