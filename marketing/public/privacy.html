Privacy

We hold records about children. This page says exactly what, why, who can see it, and how to get it back or deleted — in plain words, not boilerplate.

Last updated 18 September 2026.

Who is responsible for what

When a school uses ILM, the school decides what to record about its students and staff, and for how long. We hold and process that information on the school's instructions — in data-protection language, the school is the controller and we are the processor. Questions about a particular child's record are answered fastest by the school office; we will always help the school answer them.

For families using the family app directly, without a school, the parent is in charge of their own family's records and we hold them on that parent's instructions.

What we store

About students

  • Name, GR/registration number, class and section, admission and leaving dates
  • Date of birth and gender, where the school records them
  • Attendance, marks and exam results, homework and assignment submissions
  • Behaviour notes written by teachers, including concerns
  • Quran progress — sabaq, sabqi, manzil, nazra reading position, exam portions
  • Fees charged and payments recorded against them
  • Optionally, if the school chooses to record them: a photograph, medical or psychological conditions, blood group, religion, nationality, home language, previous school, and notes about prior suspensions

Those optional fields exist because admission forms ask for them. A school does not have to use them, and most of them are empty in practice. Health information and similar sensitive details should only be entered where the school genuinely needs them, and they are visible to school staff who have access to student records.

About parents and guardians

  • Name, relationship to the child, phone number and email address
  • Optionally: national identity number, home address, occupation and employer details, where the school's admission form collects them
  • Messages sent to and from the school through the portal
  • A PIN used to sign in, stored only as a cryptographic hash — we cannot read it, and a forgotten PIN can only be reset, never recovered

About school staff

  • Name, email address and role, and which classes or wings they are responsible for
  • A record of what they did in the system — marks entered, attendance taken, notes written — because schools need that accountability
  • Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never visible to us

From families using the family app

  • The children a parent adds, their first names and reading level
  • Prayer, habit, chore and behaviour entries logged by the parent or the child
  • Points, rewards, quests and wishlist items

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information, ever.
  • We do not show third-party advertising, and there is no advertising or tracking network inside the app.
  • We do not use children's records to build advertising profiles.
  • We do not put analytics inside the children's part of the product.

Who can see a child's record

Access follows the person's role at the school, and the system enforces it on every request rather than only hiding buttons:

  • A class teacher sees their own sections. A subject teacher sees the sections they teach.
  • A wing in-charge sees their wing. Principals and administrators see the whole school.
  • A parent sees their own children, and nobody else's.
  • A student signing in sees their own record. Some things — teachers' internal notes, and fee information — are deliberately not shown to a student's own login.

Companies that process data for us

We use a small number of providers to run the service. They act on our instructions and are not permitted to use your information for their own purposes:

  • Supabase — database, sign-in and file storage
  • Cloudflare — serving the application and websites
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — delivering push notifications, where a school enables them
  • Anthropic — when a teacher uploads a syllabus document or photograph to be read into the system, that file is sent to Anthropic's API to extract the text. It is used to answer that one request and is not used to train models.
  • Meta / Instagram — only if a school chooses to connect an Instagram feed to its public website, and only for that public feed

How long we keep it

School records are kept for as long as the school is using ILM, because a school needs a student's history across years. When a school stops using ILM, we delete or return its data within 90 days of being asked, other than anything we are required to keep by law. A student marked as having left keeps their record so the school retains an accurate history, unless the school asks us to delete it.

Family app records are kept until the parent deletes the child, deletes the family, or asks us to remove the account.

Children

ILM is used about children, and in the family app and student portal it is used by them. We collect the minimum needed for the school or the parent to do their job, we show no advertising, and we do not profile children.

For a school, the lawful basis for holding a child's record is the school's own relationship with that family. For the family app, the parent sets up the account and controls it; a child's login only ever reaches their own information.

If you believe a child's information has been given to us without the right permission, write to hello@azality.com and we will remove it.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what we hold, ask for a correction, or ask for deletion. If your child is at a school using ILM, start with the school office — they hold the record and can act immediately. If they need us, we will help them. You can also write to us directly.

See Data deletion for how to have information removed, and what happens after you ask.

Where data is held

Our database and file storage run on Supabase's infrastructure, and the application is served through Cloudflare's global network. That means information may be processed outside your own country, including where a school in Pakistan uses a provider hosted elsewhere.

Security

Connections are encrypted in transit. Sign-in PINs are stored hashed, never in readable form. Access is enforced per request against the person's role, and actions that change records are written to an append-only history rather than silently overwritten. No system is perfect; if we ever discover a breach affecting a school's data, we will tell that school without delay.

Changes

If we change how we handle information in a way that matters, we will update this page and tell the schools using ILM. The date at the top always reflects the current version.

Contact

Write to hello@azality.com for anything on this page — a question, a correction, a copy of your data, or a complaint.
