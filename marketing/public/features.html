Features

Everything a school runs on. Nothing it doesn't.

Nine modules, one login. Each built to be filled in during a real school day — roll call in under a minute, a lesson logged before the next class walks in.

Teaching Attendance & roll call One-tap roll call per section. The overview shows who's taking it and who isn't — attendance discipline becomes visible without a single meeting.
Teaching Syllabus & lesson tracking Tick topics as they're taught. Pace is computed against the term calendar automatically — ahead, on pace, or behind, in one chip.
Teaching Homework & grading Assignments with due dates, submissions, and grading-lag tracking. Parents see "due tomorrow" the moment it's posted.
Optional program Hifz program (optional) For schools that offer Qur'an memorization: sabaq, sabqi, manzil — rounds heard, portions due tonight, and each student's 30-day picture. Built for Qaris, readable by parents.
Teaching Behavior notes Positive and negative notes per student, visible to parents the same day. Praise travels home as fast as concern.
School Timetable Week grid on desktop, day list on the phone. One source of truth for students, teachers, and parents.
School Report cards & track records Per-student report cards and per-teacher track records — the term's evidence, assembled automatically from daily logging.
Parents Parent & guardian portal A daily check-in per child showing only what needs action — so parents check the app instead of calling the office.
School Roles & permissions Principal sees the school, incharge sees their wing, teachers see their classes, parents see their children. Scoped at the server, not by promise.
Built for adoption, not surveillance

Context beats ranking.

New teacher accounts get a 42-day ramp window before any comparison. Blank rows read as "needs onboarding help," never "worst teacher." And no teacher can ever see a ranked table of their colleagues — comparison belongs only to the people accountable for the whole.

01New accounts carry a visible ramp chip for 42 days — the numbers show, the judgment waits.
02Empty data is an onboarding to-do list, not a league table of bad teachers.
03Teachers see their own record through their own pages — never a ranked view of peers.
